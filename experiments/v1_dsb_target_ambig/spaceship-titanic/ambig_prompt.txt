# Spaceship Titanic — Modeling Task

## Context

It is the year 2912. The Spaceship Titanic, an interstellar passenger liner with ~13,000 passengers en route to newly habitable exoplanets, collided with a spacetime anomaly hidden in a dust cloud. Roughly half the passengers were affected by the anomaly. The data consists of personal records recovered from the ship's damaged computer system. Each row is one passenger.

## Files (in `data/`)

- `train.csv` — labeled passenger records (6,955 rows).
- `test.csv` — held-out passenger records (1,740 rows). Same schema as train except the target prediction column is missing — you must predict it.
- `sample_submission.csv` — an example submission file format.

## Columns

- `PassengerId` — unique ID of form `gggg_pp` (group / number-within-group). Group members are often family but not always.
- `HomePlanet` — planet the passenger departed from (planet of permanent residence)
- `CryoSleep` — whether the passenger elected suspended animation for the duration; cryosleep passengers are confined to their cabins
- `Cabin` — cabin number, form `deck/num/side` where `side` is `P` (Port) or `S` (Starboard)
- `Destination` — planet the passenger is debarking to
- `Age` — age of the passenger
- `VIP` — whether the passenger paid for VIP service
- `RoomService`, `FoodCourt`, `ShoppingMall`, `Spa`, `VRDeck` — amount billed at each luxury amenity
- `Name` — first and last names of the passenger
- `Transported` — whether the passenger was transported to another dimension

## Evaluation

Predictions are evaluated on **classification accuracy** on the test set.

## Submission format

CSV with header:

```
PassengerId,<target>
0013_01,<value>
0018_01,<value>
...
```

Replace `<target>` with the column you are predicting; `<value>` is your prediction for that `PassengerId`.
